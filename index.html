WATERLOO CONCRETE TOBOGGAN

We are an engineering student team from the University of Waterloo, competing in the Great Northern Concrete Toboggan Race.

We design, build, and race a five-person toboggan, whose sliding surface is made of concrete! Each year, we join teams from across Canada to compete in the Great Northern Concrete Toboggan Race (GNCTR) - Canada's oldest and largest engineering student competition.
