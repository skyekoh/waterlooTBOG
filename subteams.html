Sub-Teams

The UW Concrete Toboggan Team boasts a wide range of sub-teams for our members to join! We encourage all members to choose the sub-team that best aligns with their personal interests.

Some are more engineering-based while others are more arts-based! Scroll further down to learn more about our amazing sub-teams!

MECHANICAL

Our mechanical team designs the steering, braking, ski attachments, and frame. This year, we’re using lightweight aluminum, adding rear suspension, and testing electromagnet brakes.

CONCRETE

The concrete team designs and tests innovative ski mixes. This year, we’re using threaded rods for pre-stressing and sawdust as a sustainable fine aggregate.

SPIRIT

The spirit team creates our technical display, team apparel, and keeps team morale high.

SUSTAINABILITY

Our sustainability team researches new materials and methods to lower emissions, winning first place at GNCTR 2023, 2024, and 2025.
