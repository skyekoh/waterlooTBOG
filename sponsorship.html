Become a Waterloo Concrete Toboggan Sponsor

The University of Waterloo Concrete Toboggan Team attracts engineering students from all disciplines and years. We offer students hands-on in a safe environment to enhance their education beyond the classroom. Our team members not only learn about concrete mix design and complex steering systems, but they also learn valuable leadership and project management skills. However, we need your support in order to reach our full potential.

GNCTR 2026 is scheduled to be held in person in London, Ontario from February 4-8th, 2026. We’re excited to showcase our team’s innovation and hard work at this national event, but we can't do it without your help! Please see last year's sponsorship package below, updated information coming soon.
